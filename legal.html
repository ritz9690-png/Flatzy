Privacy & Terms

Flatzy Nagpur · Last updated: January 2025

Effective Date: 1st January 2025 · Flatzy Nagpur
🛡️ Your privacy matters to us. Flatzy collects only the information needed to help you find a home. We never sell your data to third parties.

📋 1. Information We Collect

When you use Flatzy, we may collect:

  • Google Account Info: Name, email, and profile photo when you sign in with Google.
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, properties viewed, search filters used.
  • Contact Info: Phone number if you register as a referrer or property owner.
  • Property Listings: Details, photos, and contact info submitted by owners.

🎯 2. How We Use Your Information

  • To show you relevant property listings in Nagpur.
  • To allow property owners and tenants to connect directly.
  • To process referral rewards and track referral activity.
  • To improve the Flatzy platform and user experience.
  • To send important updates about your listings or account.

🔗 3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We share only in these cases:

  • Owner/Renant Contact: When you reveal a number, that interaction is direct between both parties.
  • Firebase (Google): Our database and auth are powered by Google Firebase.
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law or government authority.

🍪 4. Cookies & Local Storage

Flatzy uses browser local storage to remember your login session and preferences. We do not use third-party advertising cookies.

🔐 5. Data Security

Your data is stored securely on Google Firebase servers with industry-standard encryption. We use Google Sign-In so no passwords are stored on our servers.

👤 6. Your Rights

  • You can request deletion of your account and data at any time.
  • You can update your profile through your Google account.
  • You can opt out by contacting us on WhatsApp.
